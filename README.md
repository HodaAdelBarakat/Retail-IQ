## 1. Executive Overview

RetailIQ is a modular, AI-powered **Prescriptive Analytics and Decision Support System** designed to identify hidden financial losses, detect operational anomalies, and support smarter retail management decisions.

Unlike traditional dashboards that primarily describe historical performance, RetailIQ combines data engineering, statistical modeling, forecasting, and business rules to transform raw retail records into actionable managerial insights.

The system follows a data-driven approach to help retail businesses move beyond understanding what happened toward identifying what requires attention and what actions should be considered.

## 2. Core System Architecture

RetailIQ integrates four analytical components within its Python processing engine.

### 2.1 Smart Data Engineering (ETL)

A data normalization and transformation engine designed to process diverse retail and ERP data structures.

- Standardizes inconsistent input schemas.
- Handles missing variables and data inconsistencies.
- Transforms raw records into structured analytical datasets.
- Supports reliable downstream processing.

### 2.2 Statistical Anomaly Detection (Z-Score)

Uses inferential statistics to identify unusual financial transactions and potential pricing irregularities.

- Applies Z-Score analysis.
- Flags observations exceeding the threshold of |Z| > 3.
- Highlights potential unauthorized discounts and pricing anomalies.
- Supports targeted financial auditing.

### 2.3 Time-Series Forecasting (ARIMA)

Applies the Autoregressive Integrated Moving Average (ARIMA) model to historical sales data.

- Analyzes historical sales patterns.
- Models time-dependent behavior.
- Generates a six-month sales forecast.
- Supports demand planning and inventory-related decisions.

### 2.4 Rule-Based Decision Matrix

Converts analytical outputs into business-oriented alerts and managerial directives.

- Combines statistical findings with predefined business rules.
- Generates automated textual warnings.
- Highlights transactions and performance indicators requiring investigation.
- Helps translate technical results into operational decisions.

## 3. Independent Cross-Validation: Power BI Layer

RetailIQ incorporates a separate Power BI environment to independently validate and present analytical results generated by the Python engine.

The Power BI dashboard operates independently from the Python web application, providing an additional analytical layer.

### 3.1 Independent KPI Replication

The sanitized dataset is independently analyzed in Power BI using custom DAX measures to reproduce key KPIs and anomaly classifications generated by the Python backend.

### 3.2 Mathematical Cross-Validation

Comparing the outputs of two independently implemented analytical environments provides an additional validation mechanism.

The observed alignment between Python-generated results and Power BI calculations supports the consistency of the underlying analytical logic.

### 3.3 Executive Dashboard

The Power BI layer provides an interactive, dark-premium reporting interface that allows stakeholders to explore business performance and audit analytical findings without directly interacting with the Python backend.

## 5. Strategic Business Value

RetailIQ is designed to support three major areas of retail business performance.

### Margin Protection
Identifies unusual discounts, pricing irregularities, and potential financial leakage that may affect profitability.

### Inventory Optimization
Uses sales forecasting to support demand planning and help reduce the risks associated with overstocking and stockouts.

### Operational Efficiency
Automates analytical checks and reporting workflows, reducing dependence on repetitive manual spreadsheet auditing.
